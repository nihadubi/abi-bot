Privacy Policy
Last updated: August 28, 2026 · This policy applies to the abi Discord bot.

Information we collect

The Bot stores only the following information:

  • Discord user ID (a numeric identifier)
  • Username and display name
  • Time spent in voice channels, measured in seconds
  • Dates associated with voice-activity records
  • Moderation records, including warning reasons and the relevant user and moderator IDs
  • Server configuration values, such as selected notification-channel IDs

We do not collect passwords, email addresses, phone numbers, or other sensitive personal information. The Bot uses only information made available to it by Discord.

How information is used

  • To provide voice-activity statistics within the server
  • To create leaderboards
  • To display user profiles and levels

Data sharing

We do not sell, share, or transfer stored information to third parties. Information is used only to operate the Bot for the relevant Discord server.

Data deletion

A server administrator can delete a user's voice statistics at any time with the abi sifirla @user command. Warning records can be deleted with abi clearwarn @user.

Children's privacy

The Bot is intended for users who meet Discord's minimum age requirements. We do not knowingly collect information from children under the applicable minimum age.

Contact

For privacy questions, please contact the Bot owner through the Discord server where the Bot is used.
